**Full description** (max 4000)
```
Touchline Fantasy is a Champions League fantasy football game built for groups of friends.

One rule sets it apart from classic fantasy: EVERY PLAYER BELONGS TO ONLY ONE MANAGER. Exactly one person in your league can own Haaland. Building a squad stops being a checklist and becomes a contest.

THE AUCTION
The season opens with an auction. In the blind round rival bids stay hidden; in the live round every bid is public and a late bid extends the lot by 60 seconds. You decide where to concentrate your budget.

REAL MATCHES, REAL POINTS
Your squad scores from actual Champions League fixtures — goals, assists, clean sheets, cards — and the captain you pick each week returns double.

ELIMINATION RISK
When a club goes out of the tournament, its players stop scoring. The exposure screen shows how much of your season is tied to each club. A re-auction opens after each knockout round and refunds the players you lost.

WEEKLY REPRICING
Player prices are re-rated every week from real statistics: minutes played, goal contributions, cards.

IN THE LEAGUE
• A pitch-view squad screen with captaincy and bench swaps
• Live auction, waiver pool and draft feed
• Weekly head-to-head duels with their own table
• Matchday live scoring feed
• A prediction game and league chat
• Turkish and English interface

FAIR PLAY
Extra transfers, waiver priority, last-second bid rights and hidden information such as rival budgets are never for sale. The paid pass only unlocks league capacity and presentation features.
```
